The recorded Steinway uses seven root positions and velocity layers 2 and 3. The optional fourth filename field identifies an alternate layer. Quiet pipe organ and plucked psaltery use their original acoustic recordings. Tone shaping and stereo effects occur at render time.

Recorded octave and playback register are calibrated independently. FFT and period checks found that the upright pianos, pizzicato strings, organ and psaltery recordings sound an octave above their source labels. The sampler compensates on playback while using the filename roots to preserve the intended warm register and spacing. The Steinway and concert harp already match their labeled roots. The following additions use measured concert-pitch MIDI roots in their bundled filenames.
