# Madlib

[← Producers](README.md)

**Who.** Producer of Quasimoto, Madvillain's *Madvillainy* and the Beat Konducta series. Studied for a loose, loop-oriented method, with limited usable evidence. Studied 2026-11-19 (dialogue) and 2026-11-22 (strums).

## What the sources say

- **Loose, improvised, loop-oriented**, built on the SP-303 and tape, "dirtier than the original", and varying from track to track, in contrast with Premier's consistent style (Medium's Micro-Chop series, secondary).
- **Guitar.** "Sped-up guitar strums" from Jimmy Smith and Wes Montgomery's "Mellow Mood" sit under Quasimoto, and "guitar strings slink across the tops of drum breaks" (DJBooth, secondary).
- **Gap.** Nothing reliable was found on how he handled spoken skit or dialogue samples. Do not cite him for any dialogue parameter.

## What Liminal took

Nothing in a shipped decision cites him. Two research rules draw on him and have no recorded adoption:

- a "loose" pattern family in which one dialogue loop repeats with minimal change and no grid correction, used in at most one in four performances ([craft §2.4](../dialogue-chopping/craft.md#24-madlib-partially-unverified));
- a "lagged loose loop" strum archetype (with Dilla) that repeats a fragment with little change ([strum hook craft §1.7](../lofi-production/strum-hook-craft.md#17-madlib-alchemist-griselda-dilla-pete-rock-secondary-or-gap)). It was not among the five archetypes that shipped in [D128](../../decisions/music.md#d128-strummed-hooks-played-off-the-drums-in-the-guitar-and-harp-phrases-with-one-echo-throw-2026-11-22).
