# The Alchemist

[← Producers](README.md)

**Who.** Producer for Mobb Deep, Prodigy, Freddie Gibbs and Griselda-adjacent records (see [Griselda](griselda.md)). Studied 2026-11-22, briefly, for how a simple loop becomes a beat.

## What the sources say

MusicRadar quotes him on "taking whatever sample, making a basic loop," and giving a beat "a little bit of structure, even if it's imagined structure." That is all the research found; no method detail on guitar, chopping or dialogue use.

## What Liminal took

Nothing in a shipped decision cites him. The quote supports the legitimacy of the loose-loop family: one fragment repeated with little change ([strum hook craft §1.7](../lofi-production/strum-hook-craft.md#17-madlib-alchemist-griselda-dilla-pete-rock-secondary-or-gap)). The rule there is that any lag is a fixed per-pattern offset, never per-note randomness.
