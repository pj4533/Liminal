# Lo-Fi composition and production research

[Handoff qualification and integrated results](handoff-checklist.md) · [Instrument and phrase qualification](../instruments/qualification.md)

The November 18 handoff set two practical goals for Liminal. Preserve the entire 16-bar AJ Hall performance and make the accompanying music sound composed, spacious and mixed around both the drums and the archival footage. The app owns the clock, arrangement and mix. BPM and key/scale are the controls. The audition ranges below are development starting points. The article, standard and product references describe specific mechanisms or measurements. The recorded smoke test qualifies one analyzer behavior on one signal.

## Drums, tempo and the audio clock

Build a 64-beat timeline at the chosen tempo. Assign boundaries from a shared frame origin with `round(beat × 60 × sampleRate / BPM)`. If evaluating a source resampled to an exact 64-beat frame count, partition that one frame range with shared boundaries. Never advance four times by independently rounded phrase lengths. At 48 kHz and 70 BPM, the first four endpoints at 16, 32, 48 and 64 beats are 658286, 1316571, 1974857 and 2633143 frames. The 4-bar layers may change durably inside those ranges. The current implementation quantizes one four-bar frame count for every stem and allocates four such spans to the intact drum buffer. That shared grid differs from an ideal 64-beat endpoint by at most two samples per take, with zero drift between stems. The source drum read is a single monotonic trip through the stereo recording with the same interpolation phase for both channels, even if the scheduler hands audio to the engine in blocks. Keep fades for genuine start/stop or explicit joins. A phrase boundary needs no gain envelope on the drum path. Capture a continuous take and compare that path with a single uninterrupted source conversion, including the last fill. For an indefinitely repeating constant tempo derive boundaries from absolute beat positions or carry the fractional remainder between breaks. Restart that origin coherently at a prepared tempo change.

Separate the metrical beat map from a kick onset map. Ellis's beat tracker uses a tempo prior and an onset-strength signal to locate perceptual beats, which are broader than individual drum attacks. For kick-aware bass, use the original close mic when licensed and available, or a conservative mixed-break detector. A practical mixed-break proxy is short-frame positive low-band energy flux with an adaptive local floor, a minimum onset spacing, and a confidence that drops on weak or ambiguous events. Follow a strong bass arrival with a tiny coherent offset, skip the note, or reduce its level briefly. Measure the offset and audition the groove. Preserve the drum recording's microtiming at every beat. Align actual onset and nominal beat publications to the scheduled/audible time, including engine latency, before driving visual effects. [Ellis, Beat Tracking by Dynamic Programming](https://www.ee.columbia.edu/~dpwe/pubs/Ellis07-beattrack.pdf)

Stereo-linked varispeed preserves sample order and shifts pitch along with tempo. The expected pitch shift is `12 × log2(playbackBPM / sourceBPM)` semitones. Matching the break to a nearby BPM reduces pitch change and the stress on re-sampling. When speeding up a bright recording, measure aliasing from the converter against a band-limited reference. A pitch-preserving stretch is a candidate comparison. Test transient shape, stereo image and fill timing on the same break before changing the default. For footage, a beat-relative echo preserves the original syllable's phase relative to the beat. At 70 BPM the eighth, dotted eighth and quarter intervals are about 429, 643 and 857 ms. If a cue needs a phase-locked entrance, align the whole cue with preroll. Leave its internal speech timing intact.

## An arrangement with memory and space

- **Choose rhythm and destinations separately.** Start with an onset mask containing intentional rests, and choose the destination before choosing passing tones. Toussaint's Euclidean construction evenly distributes attacks over a cycle and can offer sparse placements. Vary how it relates to the drummer with rotations, restrained offsets and omissions. Pachet, Roy and Barbieri give a formal example of conditioning a finite sequence on constraints. Liminal can use a small backward reachability check or a prequalified contour. Walk back from the permitted final pitches and remove candidates that cannot reach an eligible next event within the allowed interval. Preserve a valid short motif as the fallback. [Toussaint's original rhythmic analysis](https://www-cgrl.cs.mcgill.ca/~godfried/publications/banff.pdf), [Pachet et al., Finite-Length Markov Processes with Constraints](https://ijcai.org/Proceedings/11/Papers/113.pdf)
- **Use an actual return.** Seed rhythm, harmony, articulation, timbre and effects separately so a rack change cannot silently reseed the composition. Store the onset pattern, relative pitch contour, durations and accents. A variation can omit a note or move its approach while retaining the anchor. A return restores the stored motif. Keep the instrument long enough to recognize that return. Pearce and colleagues studied melodic expectedness using learned pitch probabilities. The experiment supplies a reason to test recognizable identity and restrained surprise. Chillness and the ideal repetition count are listening questions. [Pearce et al., musical expectation](https://www.marcus-pearce.com/assets/papers/PearceEtAlNeuroImage2010.pdf)
- **Give voices different roles.** Let one shared key and harmonic plan determine eligible pitches, then rank two-voice shells by MIDI-note movement, common tones and usable register. Favor silence over doubling an already busy part. Use the acoustic palette for the main musical identity. Audition an instrument's natural attack before applying a dark filter. A monophonic bass with short click-safe ramps and quiet harmonics can keep its center and remain audible on small speakers. Organ registrations or soft FM electric keys are useful controlled palette experiments. Expand acoustic root, velocity and release coverage only when the source provenance and cost make it worthwhile. [Tymoczko, The Geometry of Musical Chords](https://www.brainmusic.org/EducationalActivitiesFolder/Tymoczko_chords2006.pdf), [Chowning, The Synthesis of Complex Audio Spectra by Means of Frequency Modulation](https://web.uvic.ca/~aschloss/course_mat/MU307/MUS307_MATERIALS/Chowning_FM.pdf), [bundled instrument sample credits](../../lofi-samples.md)
- **Budget tails as part of the score.** An articulation, release and existing effect return can continue into a declared rest. Allocate a dedicated foreground phrase, reduce the sends in advance when the clip timing is available, and damp already circulating returns with a separate, smooth envelope when footage is active. Energy in a voice-like band is a practical detector. Test synthetic non-speech in that band so it remains accurately described as activity. Stable source positions aid selective attention in Best et al.'s competing speech task. Liminal's analogous stable instrument position and audible handoff need a musical listening test. For an example 16-bar pass, establish a three- to five-attack motif in bars 1–2, leave bar 3 open, use bars 5–8 as a footage-first window, make one motif alteration in bars 9–10, restore it in 13–14, and leave 15–16 for drums and the transition. [Best et al., Object continuity enhances selective auditory attention](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC2529120/)

## Routing and automatic mixing

| Path | Starting design | Verification |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Drums | Full stereo break, conservative source trim, gentle cleanup only as needed. | Null test the prepared source against the entire scheduled drum stream before mix processing. Compare first transient and final fill. |
| Bass | Mono weight, modest harmonic component, its own ducking. A prototype may recover from 1–4 dB of kick-triggered reduction over 80–180 ms. | Kick/bass impulse and overlapping low notes, with and without a known detector signal. Test for click, lost weight and pumping. |
| Dry instruments and texture | Keep lead, support and texture occupancy distinct. Clean up only where a role overlaps. Feed effects from intentional attacks or stems. | Tail-aware rest checks, matched-level attack listening, mono and stereo captures. |
| Musical wet returns | Independent sends and returns. Brightness shaping and feedback controls apply to the return. Duck current wet energy during foreground footage, separately from reducing new excitation. | Isolated and repeated impulses, foreground entry during an existing tail, postroll, phase and quiet-window energy. |
| Footage and its FX | Keep direct intelligibility and delayed ambience easy to balance. Sync delay lengths to the common tempo. | Verify stereo routing and delay tap offsets at several BPMs, then listen to actual dialogue and nonverbal clips in the combined mix. |
| Combined master | Optional mild glue compression, cautious trim, then peak protection. Measure the summed output including footage and every return. | Meter after the last output stage and rate conversion. Capture actual reduction, peak, loudness and timing on simultaneous sources. |

A compressor has a static gain law and a dynamic detector. Define the detector, stereo link, threshold, ratio, knee, attack/release and any makeup gain explicitly when those settings are available. For an owned soft-knee curve above the high knee edge, the output in dB is `threshold + (input − threshold) / ratio`. Calculate reduction separately and smooth it with the selected time constants. Document the convention, since vendor controls can use different definitions. Use the maximum channel magnitude for a linked peak detector or qualify a linked-energy detector. Summing L+R before detecting allows antiphase inputs to cancel. Start with manual calibration and little gain reduction. Test a level step and a separate sidechain signal across multiple sample rates, block patterns and polarities. Apple's `headRoom` parameter alone does not declare a threshold ratio relationship. [Giannoulis, Massberg and Reiss, Digital Dynamic Range Compressor Design](https://joshreiss.github.io/documents/2012/GiannoulisMassbergReiss-dynamicrangecompression-JAES2012.pdf)

For measured loudness, BS.1770 defines K-weighted, channel-weighted energy and gated integration. Tech 3341 uses a 400 ms momentary window and a 3-second short-term window, and specifies 400 ms measurement blocks with 75% overlap and the absolute and relative gates for integrated loudness. Loudness range describes a short-term distribution. Sample peaks, envelope crest factor and programme loudness describe different properties. For a 16- or 32-bar combined comparison, audition −20, −18 and −16 LUFS from identical material and start with a −1.5 dBTP ceiling as a hypothesis. Avoid changing the trim on every momentary window. A future calibrator can limit trim changes to ±1 dB per prepared phrase, avoid raising silence, log the chosen gains, and remeasure because a gain adjustment can change the gate membership. A rare peak catch is healthy. Recurrent reduction beyond 1–2 dB is a reason to revisit the upstream balance. These are candidate playback settings rather than standards targets. [ITU-R BS.1770-5](https://www.itu.int/rec/R-REC-BS.1770-5-202311-I/en), [EBU Tech 3341](https://tech.ebu.ch/docs/tech/tech3341.pdf), [EBU Tech 3342](https://tech.ebu.ch/docs/tech/tech3342.pdf)

Establish a peak headroom fallback before choosing a true-peak limiter. The Hämäläinen design explains how peak-envelope smoothing can release too early for delayed programme audio. A true-peak implementation additionally needs output reconstruction analysis. Looking at an oversampled input detector still leaves the processed output, final conversion and any post-stage boost to verify. Exercise single-sample bursts, closely spaced transients, phase-offset high-frequency waves, low bass, antiphase stereo, multiple block sizes and near-threshold gain. Use an independent reconstruction reference and the EBU material before asserting a dBTP ceiling. Pro-L 2 is a potential matched-loudness reference. Its manual describes true-peak limiting and oversampling and does not certify a Liminal graph. [Hämäläinen, Smoothing of the Control Signal without Clipped Output in Digital Peak Limiters](https://users.aalto.fi/~hamalap5/dafx2002/dafx_hamalainen.pdf), [ITU Annex 2](https://www.itu.int/dms_pubrec/itu-r/rec/bs/R-REC-BS.1770-5-202311-I%21%21PDF-E.pdf), [EBU loudness test set](https://tech.ebu.ch/publications/ebu_loudness_test_set), [FabFilter true-peak guide](https://www.fabfilter.com/help/pro-l/using/truepeaklimiting), [FabFilter oversampling guide](https://www.fabfilter.com/help/pro-l/using/oversampling)

## Effects with a tail budget

- **Echo and room.** Meter a short room, a dark rhythmic tap return and occasional longer atmospheric gestures separately where possible. For an ideal scalar loop with feedback amplitude `g` and delay `D` in seconds, the estimate `T60 = −3D/log10(abs(g))` follows from the decay per traversal. At `D = 0.643` seconds and `g = 0.35`, that is about 4.2 seconds, enough to play across much of a rest. In a filtered, cross-fed or modulated loop, verify the complete loop and the decay of individual bands. Consider fifths or octaves on sparse echo events with return filtering to keep the top end soft. Fewer pitch-shifted echoes can stand out more readily than a continuous shimmer wash. For tempo changes, keep the old tail alive while starting a new tempo tap. Fractional-delay read-rate changes intentionally bend pitch. [Smith, Feedback Delay Network Stability](https://www.dsprelated.com/freebooks/pasp/FDN_Stability.html), [Smith, Delay-Line and Signal Interpolation](https://www.dsprelated.com/freebooks/pasp/Delay_Line_Signal_Interpolation.html)
- **Grains, diffusion and resonance.** Clouds combines enveloped grains from recorded memory with a separate all-pass diffuser and reverb. Beads adds timed triggering and per-grain behavior. Rings demonstrates shaping pitched resonance from a short excitation. For Liminal, take a selected note or return fragment, make one quiet pitched or reversed reply, and put an explicit voice count and time/energy cap on the gesture. Seed the rack independently and reserve long or bright events for holes in the arrangement. The manuals establish intended behavior. The linked source exposes the diffuser and reverb structures. Preserve upstream notices if later importing substantial source. [Mutable Clouds manual](https://pichenettes.github.io/mutable-instruments-documentation/modules/clouds/manual/), [Beads manual](https://pichenettes.github.io/mutable-instruments-documentation/modules/beads/manual/), [Rings manual](https://pichenettes.github.io/mutable-instruments-documentation/modules/rings/manual/), [Clouds diffuser code](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/pichenettes/eurorack/master/clouds/dsp/fx/diffuser.h), [Clouds reverb code](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/pichenettes/eurorack/master/clouds/dsp/fx/reverb.h)
- **Filters and tone.** Verify static cleanup with a low-level sweep at production sample rates. For moving tone use a stable realization, smooth the physical parameters and bound cutoff/Q/gain. A safe static biquad response alone does not establish safe coefficient modulation. Measure the full trajectory for unexpected gain, harsh resonances and nonfinite output. Prefer slow correlated movement shared by part of a rack so modulation sounds coherent. Finish with attack clarity and long-term fatigue checks at matched loudness. [Bristow-Johnson Audio EQ Cookbook](https://webaudio.github.io/Audio-EQ-Cookbook/audio-eq-cookbook.html), [Wishnick, Time-Varying Filters for Musical Applications](https://www.dafx.de/paper-archive/2014/dafx14_aaron_wishnick_time_varying_filters_for_.pdf)
- **Color and tape.** Keep the bass fundamental centered and relatively clean while comparing harmonics in a parallel branch. Nonlinear drive adds harmonics above Nyquist. Audition moderate drive and measure aliases against a high-rate reference before deciding between oversampling and antiderivative antialiasing. An ADAA difference quotient needs a stable near-equal-input limit and introduces a timing/response difference in parallel paths. For an original tape-like recipe, combine restrained saturation, mellow loss shaping and fractional-delay wow/flutter. The delay movement's derivative sets speed deviation. Model-based magnetic hysteresis is a separate experiment with an oversampling cost. [Parker et al., Reducing the Aliasing of Nonlinear Waveshaping Using Continuous-Time Convolution](https://www.dafx.de/paper-archive/2016/dafxpapers/20-DAFx-16_paper_41-PN.pdf), [Chowdhury, Real-Time Physical Modelling for Analog Tape Machines](https://www.dafx.de/paper-archive/2019/DAFx2019_paper_3.pdf)

## Current playback after the Mac auditions

The 32-bar form gives each 16-bar take a piano hook and a reprise with the same pitches, onsets, durations, accents and articulation. Independent seeded choices select a two-cell rhythm and a contour ending on the tonic. The variation keeps the opening cell and changes the route home. The four-note breakdown leaves the midrange shells silent. Plucks and organ take the two middle phrases. The acoustic sample bank adds a quiet organ, plucked psaltery and a second recorded velocity layer across the Steinway map. The sample credits record the underlying public-domain dedication and note preparation. [Lo-Fi sample credits](../../lofi-samples.md)

Bass attacks follow strong kick candidates within 0.075 beats of an existing entrance. Other figures retain their scored timing. The bass duck listens to the existing drum recording through a 35–112 Hz linked detector, with a 4 ms reduction entry and 135 ms recovery and up to about 3.1 dB of reduction. The generated music uses the same arrangement and levels whether footage audio is active or quiet. The darker room recipes use a contractive feedback network, with a 2.5-second plate and 4.8–7.8 seconds of nominal hall decay by recipe. The archive delay rotates through three-tap patterns on the four-bar clock. Two banks keep the outgoing tail at its original delay spacing while a prepared bank takes over.

For melody and mids, the rack samples a short window of its own colored return, replays up to three fragments on the grid, and routes them through damping, echoes, and diffuse room sends. The final lead onset sets the capture window and the three replay entrances. The overall wet upper band has additional low-pass damping around 3 kHz and linked peak control. This combines Beads' rolling-buffer idea, Marbles' clocked variation, and Backmask's beat-relative reverse placement. [Mutable Instruments Beads](https://pichenettes.github.io/mutable-instruments-documentation/modules/beads/manual/) · [Mutable Instruments Marbles](https://pichenettes.github.io/mutable-instruments-documentation/modules/marbles/manual/) · [Freakshow Industries alignment notes](https://freakshowindustries.com/support)

The live master meter observes K-weighted stereo output after the limiter and output trim. It uses overlapping 400 ms windows and updates on 16-bar-length epochs. Its initial aim is −18 LUFS. It holds trim during sparse/quiet epochs, steps by at most 1 dB and bounds the adjustment around nominal trim to −3 through +2 dB. The master adds a nominal +3.3 dB before the system sample limiter and −4.8 dB after it. Splitting the trim this way retains the same approximate low-level loss while increasing the headroom retained by the limiter on stress input. Measure the actual true peak of every captured case after both stages.

## Evidence from a repeatable render

Capture synchronized dry roles, sends, wet returns, footage, pre-master and final PCM during the same graph execution. Shared dynamics and nonlinear processing depend on summed inputs. Solo renders serve component diagnosis. Record capture loss independently of an audio underrun. Write files and compute plots away from the real-time callback. Compare offline and real-time modes separately because provider contracts may permit more expensive offline rendering. Use different STFT windows for onsets and separation of low partials. At 48 kHz a 512-point window spans 10.7 ms, 2048 spans 42.7 ms and 8192 spans 170.7 ms. Hold axes, scales, hop, window, boundary convention and color floor fixed for comparisons. Compute energy/concurrency alongside plots and preserve original captures. A per-band ratio of speech to combined background power can indicate a collision. Publish it only where the speech reference has audio. STOI is optional for synthetic tests with clean aligned speech and comparable levels. Treat it in the context of the published experiments. Use expendable deliberately broken fixtures to show detector disconnect, return decay regression, sample offset and ceiling errors make the corresponding checks fail. Capture postroll and exercise irregular block partitions before comparing supposedly deterministic files. ### Checked-in analyzer

Run the independent analyzer on a directory with the render manifest and synchronized stereo float WAV captures. It checks format and frame count, nonfinite samples, music and master presence, footage silence when disabled, the five-stem sum against the Lo-Fi bus, and the pre-master sum against the captured music and footage buses. It measures RMS, direct sample peaks and advisory activity above −50 dBFS in 50 ms windows for each bus. FFmpeg measures integrated loudness, LRA, sample peak and true peak on the final master. The default ceiling is −1.5 dBTP. The threshold applies to the measured final capture. Use a separate EBU compliance fixture run before relying on any analyzer as a reference for general signals.

```sh
python3 scripts/analyze-lofi.py --self-test
python3 scripts/analyze-lofi.py --ebu-subset
python3 scripts/analyze-lofi.py /path/to/synchronized-capture --max-true-peak -1.5
```

The run directory receives `analysis.json` with results, observed routing error, failing assertions and the reproduce command. The raw independent meter log is `ffmpeg-ebur128.log`. The self-test covers a known transient and silence, and has mutations for a routing mismatch, footage leakage, intersample ceiling failure, a silent master, nonfinite PCM and a missing manifest. The companion plotting tool writes multiresolution spectra and speech-band diagnostics. The selected analytic reference-meter fixture results and remaining target checks are recorded in the [handoff ledger](handoff-checklist.md).

### Measured intersample smoke test

On macOS with FFmpeg 8.0.1, a 48 kHz stereo 12 kHz sine with amplitude 0.99 and phase π/4 gave **−3.1 dBFS sample peak** and **−0.1 dBTP**. These are consistent with `20 log10(0.99/√2)` and `20 log10(0.99)`. Short edge fades remove a hard discontinuity from the test. This checks that the analyzer distinguishes the two observables on a signal with known maxima. It measures neither the Liminal master nor compliance of the analyzer with the full EBU suite. FFmpeg displays the true-peak summary value with a dBFS label. Its peak mode uses libswresample. [FFmpeg ebur128 reference](https://ffmpeg.org/ffmpeg-filters.html#ebur128)

```sh
ffmpeg -hide_banner -nostats -f lavfi \
  -i 'aevalsrc=0.99*sin(2*PI*12000*t+PI/4)|0.99*sin(2*PI*12000*t+PI/4):s=48000:d=4' \
  -af 'afade=t=in:d=0.1,afade=t=out:st=3.9:d=0.1,ebur128=peak=sample+true:framelog=quiet' \
  -f null -
```

For a persisted production WAV, use `ebur128=peak=sample+true:metadata=1:framelog=info` and save stderr separately from rendered PCM. The null output discards frame metadata. Use an explicit export if a machine-readable per-frame trace is needed.
